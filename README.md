### Step 2: Schedule Visits and Inspections

Once you've shortlisted a few properties, the next step is to schedule visits. During the visit, pay attention to:

- **Condition of the Property**: Look for any repairs needed or maintenance issues.
- **Neighborhood Vibe**: Explore the area, its amenities, and overall living conditions.
- **Future Developments**: Investigate any planned developments that may affect your property value.

### Step 3: Verify Legal Documentation

Before making a purchase, ensure that all legal documentation is in order:

- **Title Deed**: Confirm that the seller has a clear title to the property.
- **Encumbrance Certificate**: Check for any mortgages or liens against the property.
- **Occupancy Certificate**: Verify that the property is legally habitable.

### Step 4: Finalizing the Purchase

Once you’re satisfied with your property choice, it's time to make an offer. This process typically involves:

- **Negotiation**: Engage in discussions regarding the price and terms of sale.
- **Agreement**: Sign a sale agreement outlining all the conditions and terms agreed upon.
- **Payment Processing**: Arrange for the transfer of funds, either through a bank loan or other means.

### Step 5: Registration of Property

The final step in the purchase process is property registration. Ensure you have all required documents ready, including:

- **Sale Deed**: This legally transfers ownership.
- **ID Proofs**: Your identification and KYC documents.

Register the property at the local sub-registrar office to complete the transaction.
